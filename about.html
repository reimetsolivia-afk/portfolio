About Me

Mrs. Gremlin is an artist who also likes to write and worldbuild. I love cozy, whimsical vibes that can be both cute and slightly eerie at the same time. I also love drinking tea and spending time with my Mr. Gremlin—watching shows, reading books, and gaming.

I love both deeply emotional stories and art that make people feel something, as well as silly, less serious, fun creative things made purely from the joy of making. Everything I make falls somewhere in between: it can be an emotion or story I needed to express, or simply something fun I wanted to do because I enjoy creating.

I enjoy both traditional and digital art, though I can spend far more time on my digital pieces, and the undo button is truly a blessing. I often find it easier to let loose during the sketching process in my sketchbook, though. It can be very therapeutic and calming to hold an actual pencil, pen, or brush and bring a creation to life. The lack of an undo button can be liberating in its own way.

I love creating and imagining worlds in my mind, and drawing is a way to bring them to life. Both writing and drawing are ways to look inside myself, discover something new, and express something that might otherwise be difficult to put into words.
